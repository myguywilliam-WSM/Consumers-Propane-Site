02 — Our Story
1919

100+
Years of
Community.

Founded in 1919 by the Bousquet family as a coal dealer, Consumers has grown alongside the communities of Northern Rhode Island and Southeastern Massachusetts for over a century.

From coal to propane, from kerosene to modern HVAC, we've always adapted to serve our neighbors better. When you call us, you're calling a neighbor — not a call center.

Get in Touch
Our Timeline
1919
Founded by the Bousquet Family
Established as a coal dealer in Woonsocket, RI. From day one, family values and community service were at the core of the business.
1929
Gasoline Added
As the automobile became central to American life, Consumers expanded into gasoline delivery, broadening their fuel services across the region.
1942
Heating Oil Services Launch
Consumers added heating oil, responding to the shift away from coal as fuel technology evolved and communities needed modern solutions.
1956
Propane Division Established
Propane opened up service to rural customers, expanding the company's reach across RI and into MA with a modern, clean-burning fuel option.
Now
Full-Service Energy & HVAC
4th generation family ownership. Propane, oil, gasoline, kerosene, diesel, and complete HVAC — a one-stop energy provider built on a century of trust.
