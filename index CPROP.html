Our Story

100+ Years of Serving Your Community

Founded in 1919 by the Bousquet family as a coal dealer, Consumers has grown alongside the communities of Northern Rhode Island and Southeastern Massachusetts for over a century. The 3rd and 4th generations now carry forward a tradition of dependable service, fair prices, and genuine customer relationships.

From coal to propane, from kerosene to modern HVAC, we've always adapted to serve our neighbors better. Thousands of customers have stayed loyal for decades because they know: when you call us, you're calling a neighbor, not a call center.

Our Timeline

1919
1919
Founded by the Bousquet Family

Established as a coal dealer in Woonsocket, RI during the era when coal was the primary home heating fuel. From the very first day, family values and community service were at the core of the business.

1929
1929
Gasoline Added

As the automobile became central to American life, Consumers expanded into gasoline delivery, broadening their fuel services and customer base throughout the region.

1942
1942
Heating Oil Services Launch

During World War II, Consumers added heating oil to their portfolio, responding to the shift away from coal as fuel technology evolved and communities needed modern solutions.

1956
1956
Propane Division Established

Propane became the next frontier, opening up service to rural customers and expanding the company's reach across Rhode Island and into Massachusetts with a modern, clean-burning fuel option.

Now
Today
Full-Service Energy & HVAC

Now in its 4th generation of family ownership, Consumers offers propane, oil, gasoline, kerosene, diesel, and complete HVAC installation and service — a one-stop energy provider built on a century of trust.
